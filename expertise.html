Not sure which one you need?

Start from the constraint, not the job title.

“We need marketing” is not yet a usable brief. If qualified customers cannot discover you, look at SEO, content or awareness. If traffic exists but revenue does not, investigate conversion, offer design or lifecycle marketing. If customers arrive and leave, the constraint is retention.

You do not have to diagnose it alone—a structured brief is enough for Growza to suggest the specialism.

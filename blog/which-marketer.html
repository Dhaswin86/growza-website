Which type of marketer does your business actually need?

Start with the commercial constraint, not the job title. The specialist you need is the one whose work directly changes that constraint.

Hiring · 8 min

Choose the problem before choosing the marketer

“We need marketing” is not yet a usable brief. Begin by identifying the commercial constraint. If qualified customers cannot discover you, look at SEO, content or awareness. If traffic exists but revenue does not, investigate conversion, offer design or lifecycle marketing.

A simple rule: hire for the next meaningful bottleneck—not for every marketing activity your company may eventually need.

Five constraints and who fixes them

  1. Nobody knows you exist. SEO, content or brand awareness.
  2. They know you, but do not come. Paid acquisition or positioning.
  3. They come, but do not buy. CRO, landing pages or offer design.
  4. They buy once, then leave. Lifecycle, email and retention.
  5. They cannot tell you apart. Brand strategy and positioning.

How to test a candidate

Ask each candidate to explain the problem in their own words, show relevant proof and outline the first two weeks of work. You are evaluating their diagnosis as much as their portfolio. A specialist who reframes your problem before quoting is usually worth more than one who agrees immediately.

FAQ

Questions before you begin.

How matching works, what it costs, how quality is assessed and what happens when a project goes wrong.

What is Growza?

Growza is a focused marketplace connecting businesses with marketers, freelancers and agencies based on the business goal, relevant expertise, budget and availability.

How does matching work?

You submit a structured brief. Growza identifies suitable specialists and presents a concise shortlist with relevant work, capabilities and availability.

How much does it cost?

Both sides start free. Businesses can add Growth (₹999/month) or Scale (₹2,499/month) for more outreach and analytics; marketers can add Pro (₹899/month) or Max (₹1,799/month) for more visibility and capacity. A flat 8% success fee applies to marketer earnings on every plan.

How is specialist quality assessed?

Profiles prioritise demonstrated expertise, relevant work samples, clear service scope and client outcomes. Early matches are also reviewed manually.

What if a project goes wrong?

Growza reviews the brief, agreed milestones and delivered work, then supports a revision, mediation, re-match or partial refund where appropriate.

How long does matching take?

Most briefs return a shortlist within two working days. Highly specialised or regulated work can take longer because the pool is smaller.

Can I hire more than one specialist?

Yes. Briefs that need several disciplines can be split into separate scopes, or matched to an agency that coordinates them under one engagement.

Who owns the work produced?

You do. Ownership of deliverables transfers to the business on completion unless the two sides agree otherwise in writing before the work starts.

Hiring in Chennai

What this costs.

Starting rates on Growza Chennai run from ₹4,000 for copy and captions to ₹15,000 for tech reviews with a large audience. Most marketers price per deliverable rather than per hour, so the number you see is the floor for a defined piece of work—not a retainer.

Before you compare rates: a lower rate on a vague scope costs more than a higher rate on a defined one. Fix the deliverable first, then compare.

What to check

  1. Audience overlap, not audience size. 27K local followers can beat 148K scattered ones.
  2. Language. Tamil and English campaigns need different creative, not a translation.
  3. Where they are. On-location work—shoots, openings, store visits—is a travel question.
  4. What they actually deliver. Every listing states scope, revisions and turnaround up front.

Hiring a marketer in Chennai.

Do I have to hire someone in my part of Chennai?

Only for work that happens somewhere—shoots, openings, store visits. Marketers here are spread from Nungambakkam to Porur, and each profile shows its locality so you can judge the travel.

What does it cost to hire through Growza?

Posting a brief and browsing is free. Marketers pay a flat 8% success fee on what they earn. Businesses are never charged a percentage; Growth and Scale are optional subscriptions for more outreach and analytics.

How do I know a marketer is genuine?

Every profile on this page has cleared identity verification, and listings pass a content review before they go live. Your payment is held and released only when you accept the delivered work.

Can I hire more than one?

Yes. A launch that needs stills, video and copy is three scopes, not one—and they can run at the same time.

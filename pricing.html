How the money works

Subscriptions and the success fee are separate.

A subscription is what you pay Growza each month for reach, limits and insight. The success fee is separate: 8% of a marketer’s earnings, charged once, on work that has been delivered and accepted. If an order is cancelled, refunded or never accepted, there is no fee.

  • Posting a brief and browsing marketers is free on every plan.
  • Building a profile and applying to campaigns is free on every plan.
  • The 8%% success fee comes out of the marketer’s earnings, not the business’s budget.
  • Businesses are never charged a percentage—only the optional subscription.
Launch pricing. These plans are provisional while Growza is new. Anyone already subscribed keeps their price for twelve months if it changes.

Common questions

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes, on both sides. Plans change from your dashboard and take effect immediately—upgrades are prorated, downgrades apply at the next renewal.

What happens if I cancel?

You drop to the free tier at the end of the paid period. Your profile, portfolio, campaigns and history stay; only the limits and visibility change back.

Does a paid plan buy better ranking?

It raises your search visibility, it does not buy the top spot. Relevance and delivered work still decide the order—otherwise a shortlist would stop being worth reading.

Who pays the success fee?

The marketer. The amount a business agrees to is the amount it pays; the 8%% comes out of the payout once the buyer accepts the work.

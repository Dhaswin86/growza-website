How to record an MP4

There is no ffmpeg on this machine, so the two animated cuts are pages rather than video files. To get an MP4:

  1. Open a cut full screen and let it load
  2. Press ⇧⌘5, choose Record Selected Portion
  3. Drag around the stage, hit Record, then click ↻ Replay
  4. Stop when the logo fades — macOS saves a .mov you can share or convert

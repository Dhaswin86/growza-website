Why Growza

Hiring a marketer should not be a leap of faith.

Growza is built around the parts that usually go wrong: who you can trust, what you are paying for, and what happens when the work is not right.

Six reasons

What makes this different.

Not features—the guarantees underneath them.

✓

Nobody reaches you unverified

Identity is checked before a listing can be bought. A profile you can see is a profile that has already cleared the gate—signing up is not enough.

₹

You pay for work, not for access

Starter is free on both sides. The fee applies once, to work that has been delivered and accepted, and never to work that is cancelled or refunded.

◎

A shortlist, not a directory

Matching starts from the constraint holding growth back. Placement comes from relevance and delivered work—not from who paid to rank.

⌗

Scope is agreed before money moves

Deliverables, milestones and success measures are fixed at the order, so the work is judged on what both sides signed up to rather than on expectations invented later.

↻

Accepting is what pays

Your payment is held until you accept the delivery. A specialist can never sign off their own work, and nobody is paid twice for one order.

◈

Local where it actually matters

Shoots, openings and store visits are a travel question. Every profile shows its locality, so you can judge that before the first call.

Directories and agencies are not bad choices—they are different ones. A directory is faster when you already know exactly who you want; an agency is right when several disciplines have to move together under one accountable party.

Two flows, one match

How each side moves through Growza.

Businesses start from a problem. Specialists start from proof. Both pass the same approval checkpoints on the way to an agreed scope.

Green checkpoints are admin approvals — work does not move past them until it is cleared.

Flow A · For businesses

From problem to delivered work

  1. 1Post a brief

    Goal, budget, timeline and the constraint holding growth back—not a job title.

  2. ✓Admin approvalOnly verified specialists are matched

    Every profile in your shortlist has already cleared an identity check. Unverified accounts cannot be hired.

  3. 2Compare the shortlist

    Comparable work, starting scope, availability and price, side by side.

  4. 3Request a meeting

    Talk to the specialist about the work before committing to anything.

  5. ✓Admin approvalMeeting approved

    Meeting requests are reviewed before the time is confirmed for either side.

  6. 4Order with a brief

    Deliverables, milestones and measures are fixed. Your payment is held, not released.

  7. ✓Admin approvalDelivery reviewed

    Submitted work is checked before it reaches you. Rejections come back with a stated reason.

  8. 5Accept and release payment

    Accepting is what pays the specialist and opens the review. Nothing is released until you do.

Flow B · For specialists

From profile to paid work

  1. 1Create your profile

    Specialisms, industries and comparable work with outcomes attached. Free, permanently.

  2. 2Submit identity documents

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or licence—uploaded once, from your dashboard.

  3. ✓Admin approvalIdentity verified

    Until this clears, your listings are saved but unbuyable. This is the gate that makes a profile sellable.

  4. 3List your services

    Three packages per service, each with its own scope and price.

  5. ✓Admin approvalListing approved

    Listings pass a content review first. A rejection has to come with a reason you are shown.

  6. 4Deliver the work

    Submitted against the milestones you agreed when the order came in.

  7. ✓Admin approvalDelivery approved

    Approval decides whether the work may be published—not whether it is paid.

  8. 5Get paid on acceptance

    The business accepting releases payment. You can never sign off your own work.

↓ Both flows meet at the match ↓

One agreed scope, one set of milestones, one definition of done—shared by both sides before any money moves. That shared record is what Growza uses to support revisions, mediation or a re-match.

Approval and payment are separate. Clearing the delivery checkpoint decides whether the work may be published. The business accepting it is what releases payment and opens the review—so a specialist can never sign off their own work, and nobody is paid twice for one order.

The honest part

What Growza is not.

Growza is early. The marketplace is live in Chennai with a small number of verified marketers, matching is partly manual, and pricing is provisional. None of that is hidden behind a waitlist.

  • It is not the biggest pool. It is a checked one. If you want thousands of options, a directory will serve you better.
  • It does not replace judgement. Verification proves identity, not that someone is right for your problem.
  • It is not free for marketers at scale. The fee is real, and the paid plans lower it rather than removing it.
Why say this on a marketing page? Because the whole product is a bet that a shortlist you can trust beats a list that flatters you.

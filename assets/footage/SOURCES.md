# Promo footage

Background clips for the promotional film. All three are from **Mixkit**, whose
free licence permits commercial use with no attribution required and no account
needed. They are stored in the repo rather than hot-linked so the film plays
with no third-party request at runtime — the same reasoning as the self-hosted
fonts and photography.

| `freelancer-laptop.mp4` | Scene 1 — the freelancer, "you didn't lose the job" | https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/man-working-on-his-laptop-308/ |
| `product-shoot.mp4` | Scene 4 — the marketplace, a marketer actually working | https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/female-photographer-taking-product-photos-on-a-set-44119/ |
| `social-cafe.mp4` | Scene 6 — both sides, a cafe and a phone | https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/person-on-social-media-while-serving-coffee-4919/ |

Each sits behind a dark overlay at low opacity, so it reads as texture rather
than as the subject — the words stay the point.

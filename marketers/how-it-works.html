The path

What happens, in order.

Your journey · For marketers

From profile to paid work

  1. 1Create your profile

    Specialisms, industries and comparable work with outcomes attached. Free, permanently.

  2. 2Submit identity documents

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or licence—uploaded once, from your dashboard.

  3. ✓Admin approvalIdentity verified

    Until this clears, your listings are saved but unbuyable. This is the gate that makes a profile sellable.

  4. 3List your services

    Three packages per service, each with its own scope and price.

  5. ✓Admin approvalListing approved

    Listings pass a content review first. A rejection has to come with a reason you are shown.

  6. 4Deliver the work

    Submitted against the milestones you agreed when the order came in.

  7. ✓Admin approvalDelivery approved

    Approval decides whether the work may be published—not whether it is paid.

  8. 5Get paid on acceptance

    The business accepting releases payment. You can never sign off your own work.

Two separate decisions. Clearing the delivery checkpoint decides whether your work may be published. The business accepting it is what releases your payment—so you can never sign off your own work, and nobody is paid twice for one order.

Getting verified

What we actually check.

Verification is an identity check, not a skills exam. You upload one government document—Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence—and a moderator confirms the name and photo match the profile.

  • It happens once. Cleared, you stay verified.
  • Your listings are saved but unbuyable until it clears. Write them while you wait.
  • It is not a judgement on your work. Nobody reviews your portfolio to verify you.
Why bother? Because the badge is what makes a buyer trust a profile they have never seen before. The check protects your reputation as much as theirs.

Setting your prices

Every service carries three packages. The usual mistake is making them three sizes of the same thing. The better structure is three different commitments: a single deliverable, a small bundle, and an ongoing arrangement. Buyers self-select, and you stop negotiating scope in messages.

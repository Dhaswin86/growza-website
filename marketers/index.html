What you actually do here

Sell defined work, not your time.

Growza is not a job board and not a bidding pit. You publish services with a fixed scope and price, and businesses order them the way they would order anything else.

▤

Publish services, not availability

Each service carries three packages. You decide what is in each one, what it costs, how many revisions it includes and how long it takes. A buyer picks a package and that is the agreement—no scoping call needed to get started.

Get matched to briefs that fit

When a business posts a campaign, Growza surfaces it to marketers whose category, budget range and availability match. You are not competing with everyone—you are one of a handful who genuinely fit.

Deliver, then get paid

The buyer’s payment is held before you start. You deliver against the milestones you agreed, and the money is released when they accept. No invoicing, no chasing.

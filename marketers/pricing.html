The honest maths

When upgrading is worth it.

Because the fee never changes, a paid plan only pays for itself if it wins you work you would not otherwise have won. That is a real question, so here is the plain version:

  • Free is right until you are regularly hitting the 10-campaign application limit or running out of portfolio slots.
  • Pro (₹899) makes sense once you are applying to most weeks and want the verified badge and higher search placement working while you sleep.
  • Max (₹1,799) is for marketers treating this as their main channel—unlimited portfolio, 100 applications and featured placement.
No plan buys the top spot. Higher visibility means you surface more often among relevant results. Delivered work still decides the order.

How the 8% works

It comes out of your payout, once, on work that has been delivered and accepted. A cancelled or refunded order costs you nothing. The buyer pays exactly what they agreed—the fee is not added on top of their price.

When am I paid?

When the buyer accepts the delivery. Their payment is held from the moment they order, so the money already exists before you start.

What if a buyer never accepts?

Growza reviews the brief, the agreed milestones and what you delivered, then supports a revision, mediation or release where appropriate.

Can I cancel a plan?

Yes. You drop to Free at the end of the paid period and keep your profile, portfolio and history—only limits and visibility change back.

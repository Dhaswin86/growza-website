Getting your first ten orders

Nobody has reviewed you yet. Here is what to do about the cold-start problem.

Growth · 8 min

The cold-start problem is real

Ranking is earned by delivered work, which is a problem when you have delivered none. Nobody solves this with a better bio. You solve it by making the first few orders unusually easy to say yes to.

Four things that work

  1. Make package one small. A low-commitment entry point converts strangers.
  2. Be specific about a niche. “Reels for Chennai cafes” beats “video” when there are already people doing video.
  3. Apply to briefs you actually fit. A considered application to five right-fit campaigns beats forty scattergun ones.
  4. Over-deliver on the first three. Reviews compound; the first ones are worth more than the money.
Do not discount to zero. A free tier of your own trains buyers to expect it. Make the first package small, not worthless.

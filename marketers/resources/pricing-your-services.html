How to price your three packages

Three sizes of the same thing is the common mistake. Three different commitments is the fix.

Pricing · 7 min

Three commitments, not three sizes

The default instinct is to build Basic, Standard and Premium as small, medium and large of the same deliverable. It rarely works, because a buyer who wants one reel does not want three reels—they want one reel plus the things that make it land.

A better structure: package one is a single deliverable. Package two is that deliverable plus the work that makes it perform. Package three is an ongoing arrangement.

Work backwards from your floor

Decide the least you will accept for a day of your attention, then price package one so it clears that after the 8% fee. If the number feels uncomfortably high, it is probably close to right—most people underprice the first package and then resent the work.

Put the limits in writing

Revisions, turnaround and what is excluded belong in the package, not in a message thread afterwards. A package that says “two revisions, five working days, does not include paid promotion” is doing the negotiating for you before the order arrives.

Writing a profile that wins work

Buyers are not reading your biography. They are looking for evidence you have solved their problem before.

Profile · 6 min

Lead with the problem you solve

A buyer scanning profiles is not comparing biographies. They are asking one question: has this person done my thing before? Your first line should answer it—“short-form video for cafes and restaurants” beats “creative professional with a passion for storytelling” every time.

Show outcomes, not just output

A gallery proves you can make things. A caption proves you understood why. Attach the context: what the business needed, what you made, and what happened next.

Specificity beats volume. Six relevant pieces with context will win more work than thirty assorted ones without it.

Get verified early

The verification badge is often the difference between a profile a buyer trusts and one they scroll past. It is an identity check, not a skills exam, and it only has to happen once.

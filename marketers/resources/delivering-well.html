Delivering work that gets accepted first time

Acceptance is what pays you, so the delivery itself is part of the product.

Delivery · 5 min

Acceptance is the finish line

On Growza the buyer accepting the delivery is what releases your payment. That makes the handover part of the product, not an afterthought—work that is technically complete but confusing to receive sits unaccepted.

Deliver against the package, explicitly

Restate what the package promised and show each item against it. A buyer who can tick off what they paid for accepts in minutes; one who has to work it out asks questions instead.

Name what is not included. Reminding them at handover prevents the “can you just also…” that turns an accepted order into a week of unpaid revisions.

If something goes wrong

Say so early and propose the fix. Growza reviews the brief, the agreed milestones and what was delivered, then supports a revision, mediation or release. A problem raised early is a revision; the same problem raised at the deadline is a dispute.
